          About the conference

          More than an annual event

          Edu Women in Leadership is a growing professional community committed to women’s leadership, educational transformation, innovation, collaboration, and sustainable school improvement.

          Why we gather

          The conference gives women in education a platform to strengthen their leadership capacity, exchange ideas, build professional networks, showcase innovation, address the challenges facing schools and communities, and shape the future of education.

          In 2027 we meet in Durban on 27–28 August under the theme The Future Belongs to Bold Leaders: Women Reimagining Education for a Changing World, with participation from all nine South African provinces, across Africa and from international education partners.
